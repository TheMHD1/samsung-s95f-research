## Global selector and RTP category are separate axes

Every retained GJ recorder R endpoint and logger-control R contains `R+0x10=4`. This sampled global operating-mode selector is compatible with RTP formatter/predicate category1; it is not a contradiction or a requirement for global1. The selected image1 source loads global root+2410 at `bcb2/bcb6`, branches on4 at `bcb8`, and supplies formatter category1 at `bcd0/bcd2→c5d0`. Independently, AA0/AC2 selects predicate argument1 at `bfda/bfdc`; AA1 selects4 and AC1 selects6. These are source-conditional mappings, not executing-register or caller observations.

FA's historical “Mode1 aa0/ac2” wording describes that dispatch/context shorthand. Its exact static profile has R+0x10=4; FN checks AA/AC separately and does not require globalR16=1.
