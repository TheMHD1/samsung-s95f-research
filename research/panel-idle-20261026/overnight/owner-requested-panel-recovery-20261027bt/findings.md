# BT operational recovery with For You selected

The owner requested an urgent panel restart and wake attempt. The public
preflight showed upgrade 0, display lcdon, screen state 1 and burn-in-in-progress
0 on the known Linux boot. An initial host parser rejected Int32 spelling
before controller dispatch; the correction is retained. One unchanged stock
MODE2 helper then returned accepted and cleaned up. The subsequent WS wake
attempt timed out, so its dispatch is not proved. Cached UI remained For You.

The owner reported that everything was black. A manual off/on showed For You
briefly, then black again. No physical recovery is established for this trial.
BU later selected populated Apps first and recovered with one stock MODE2.
These separate trials do not distinguish immediate recurrence from ineffective
physical recovery in BT. No scientific bracket was repaired or retried here.
