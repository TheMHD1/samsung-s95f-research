# Independent control-v3 runtime audit

## Conclusion

No transport, artifact-integrity, native state-machine, or rollback defect was
found in the recovered control-v3 evidence.

The recovered **native control** is accepted: it executed the fixed finite
sequence once, verified the volatile logger gate `0 -> 1 -> 0`, restored the
entire sampled RT block exactly, released and closed the USB interface, and
ended its managed protocol model at `ReadyForHeader` with no reported error.

That conclusion is separate from the original **host wrapper** result. The
original wrapper stopped while parsing the malformed launch receipt. It did
not observe READY live, did not issue a UI action, did not collect physical
panel feedback, and did not clean the retained stage. The later filesystem
fetch recovered the already-completed native result; it did not execute the
native helper or send another controller request.

## Read-only transport and reconstruction

The complete retrieval contains exactly 17 sequential authenticated root
calls: two terminal metadata snapshots and 15 bounded artifact-chunk reads.
For every call I verified:

- the recorded command exactly equals the command regenerated by the reviewed
  empty-chunk-fixed fetch helper;
- `command_bytes` is exact, with a maximum of 6,635 bytes;
- the decoded raw transport response exactly equals the retained parsed
  response;
- authentication is true, UID and EUID are zero, and there is exactly one
  command result;
- transport and command exit codes are zero, neither layer timed out, and both
  stderr fields are empty;
- the command echo equals the requested command and its stdout equals the
  corresponding retained `raw-call-NNN.stdout.txt` byte-for-byte.

The two terminal snapshots report the same 14-file inventory, artifact sizes
and hashes, completion record, native-exit bytes, supervisor identity, and
absent supervisor/native processes. Their target uptimes advance from
`115201.99` to `115204.78`. Each artifact was reconstructed independently from
its authenticated chunk envelope and matches the separately retained file in
size and SHA-256. The earlier parser-stopped read at uptime `115069.43` reports
the same files, completion, process states, and native-exit bytes; only its
zero-length host parser rejected the valid seven-line empty-data envelope.

All retrieval commands are filesystem/process reads. They contain no native
launch, device access, controller transfer, reset, signal, removal, or cleanup.
The second retrieval is a new read-only artifact fetch after the host-parser
stop, not a retry of the native/controller operation.

Both saved launch diagnostics are empty. The completion record binds supervisor

The fetched READY record has the exact schema, stage token, boot ID, child PID

`ready-published` at 168 ms, and `hold-start` at 170 ms. This proves the native
process published the retained READY artifact; it does not turn it into a live
host READY observation during the original run.

## Native sequence and rollback

The one JSON outcome in `native.stdout` passes the previously reviewed semantic
validator. Four other lines are classified GC diagnostics. My independent
check found:

- `Stage=complete`, `Accepted=true`, child exit `0`, no errors, and no deadline
  violation;
- exactly 16 ordered transfer steps with the expected name, direction, size,
  before/after phase, exact byte count, and errno zero;
- callback, baseline ring, pre-arm RT, arm, armed RT, restore, restored RT, and
  post-ring operations occur in the fixed order;
- open, claim, release, close, arm verification, observation completion,
  restore transport, and restore verification are all true;
- `RestoreUnverified=false` and `LogFlagMayRemainChanged=false`;
- `PreArmGate=0`, `ArmedGate=1`, `RestoredGate=0`, and `PreArmFault=0`;
- the pre-arm and restored 64-byte RT blocks are byte-identical with SHA-256
  `eed5edc3c31936e3d9612360ac9b994ba086e90378f6b8cfa0ce4e89524a0568`;
- the armed RT block differs from them at only byte offset 6, changing `00` to
  `01`;
- the 64-byte callback block exactly matches the earlier independently captured
  live DMA-alias callback block;
- the fixed hold markers span exactly 20,000 ms; target uptime advances 20.08 s
  between observation endpoints; restore is verified at 20,176 ms and the
  post-ring sample completes at 20,178 ms.

These observations support `ReadyForHeader` at the end of this managed
transaction. They do not constitute a timeless or system-wide guarantee about
the controller parser after unknown future actors or traffic.

## Remaining limits

- No physical panel state or picture transition was observed.
- The result does not establish a repair, explain the OLED/panel fault, or show

  despite the independently recovered successful native completion.
