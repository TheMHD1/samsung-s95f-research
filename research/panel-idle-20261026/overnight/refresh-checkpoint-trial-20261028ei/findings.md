## Logger limits and next diagnostic

EH preserves DP's eight reads and original34-byte record prefix, appending
the trainer controls, selector mirror, refresh counter, two end-R writer
controls and dynamic A/B pointer words. It uses54-byte records, at most60
cycles and a15-second cooperative observation budget at250ms cadence. Its
first actual attempt is an initial-fault capture, not a successful60-cycle
trace. The original successful-trial guard remains intact.

There is a concrete missing window before EH: return-to-Apps, idle,
screensaver/wake and header preparation. None is identified as this fault's
onset. Future observers should include those transitions or use separately
qualified directional workflows. Faster sampling or an owned Linux-process
sidecar may retain more data, but neither is implemented by EH. An execution
hook still lacks proved controller scratch/install/rollback ownership.

A single-lane eligibility-bit diagnostic is being designed separately to
test a precise comparator contribution. No lane bit, secondary flag,
threshold, instruction, kernel, boot setting or flash image was changed
by this sequence. Apps remains the last cached selection; emitted light is
unobserved. Recovery establishes the sampled software state, not permanent
prevention, physical repair or instruction reload.
