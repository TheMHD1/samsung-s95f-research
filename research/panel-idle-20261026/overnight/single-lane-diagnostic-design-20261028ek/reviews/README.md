## Packet and source meaning

The one fixed address is CPU`0x20010000 + 2*570 = 0x20010474`, bus`0x47010474`. It is an aligned two-byte interval within the source-qualified image1 SRAM alias, not a code-bank, flash or boot-storage address. The primary136-byte read starts at bus`0x47010470`, so the word is at offset4. The secondary lane byte is CPU`0x2000c95c+570`, offset2 of the existing68-byte secondary read. The header independently decodes to little-endian u32 `(0x102,0x47010474,2)`. Set data is u16`1`, restore data is the retained u16`0`; no address, length or value is caller-selectable in this design.

The actual primary test is a **byte load** followed by `&1`, at selected image1`bf22/bf26/bf2a`, for the third lane of a four-lane group. With group base568, it tests lane570 and skips to`bf62` when set. It precedes the secondary/SB test for this lane. Thus a verified word0→1 changes the local skip predicate irrespective of SB, conditional on this code, root and lane being reached. It is not proof of a physical-pixel mapping, sensor failure or whole-protection bypass. An earlier comparator can exit the loop, and skipping one early return can expose later comparators; aggregate score is not guaranteed to fall by1.

EC establishes that the primary word is a stock publication of `(secondary + 2*counter) modulo65536`; its other15bits are owned data. Requiring the **entire original word0**, the exact installed word1 and equality of the surrounding primary/secondary windows is stronger than just checking bit0. It correctly refuses to overwrite a different publication. Counter wrap means primary0 is not proof of empty history. Secondary0 is separately checked and never cleared by this proposal.

The five new code/literal read regions total324bytes. Their lengths, exact read headers and hashes match the selected package. The literal adjunct is necessary: body hashes alone would not measure all PC-relative literal values. Historical DX/DY samples do not cover these later local gates/literals. A new read-only positive qualification is the smallest useful next target acquisition; it establishes sampled source correspondence, not active instruction fetch or complete executing-image identity.

## Practical known-writer and ownership scope

The sampled controls disable the known selected-source producer families: trainer CPU4a2 must be0; the alternate update path requires root2460==1; primary publication is additionally gated by root246e and the documented trainer/path state. All are required0, together with refresh counter0, settled coherent selector/mirror, unchanged root/geometry/dispatch and full table-window equality. These are meaningful, testable exclusions of **known source paths at sampled times**. They are not a continuous lock, a proof against a previously entered writer, an all-core/DMA writer census or an atomic compare-and-swap.

The critical indistinguishable case is stock publication of the same word1 while the diagnostic owns1. The fresh word and all retained sampled guards can look identical. A later restore0 could erase that stock state. No number of equal readbacks can distinguish two identical writes without an independent generation/history observation. The bounded model explicitly accepts this case and reports that limitation. This is a residual ownership risk; it is not evidence that such a stock write happened.

An actionable qualification need not demand impossible absolute absence of every writer. It should identify the known activation/clear/publication paths and their observable controls, test stability over the short planned admission/exposure/restore interval, exclude other coordinated clients and automatic UI/reset activity, retain all full-window differences and source correspondence, and explicitly bound the remaining unobserved same-value/race risk. More samples reduce missed observable transitions but cannot turn the result into a lock. If the remaining risk is accepted for a short, single-lane runtime diagnostic, it must be recorded as such rather than labeled exclusive ownership. No blanket all-lane bypass or persisted/high-bank/POFLG route follows from this review.

## Unknown phase: concrete effect and strict stop invariant

The independent source check follows selected image0, file offset`VA+0x200`, beyond the inherited host model:

* Validator`29284..29296` rejects received headers shorter12; `28f68..28f70` exits the dispatch on that refusal. `292e8..292ee` admits0x102. Nonzero family1 selects the unremapped bus address; operation normalization produces2, whose dispatcher at`2912a..29148` passes the destination to OUT scheduler`7fce`.
* Scheduler`7fea/7fec` retains destination and requested length. **The OUT path at`2ac90..2aca8` rounds descriptor capacity up to an endpoint packet**, not merely the requested2bytes. Descriptor builder`296e/2970` sets the destination+offset. For maxpacket512, a requested2-byte transfer arms capacity512. This is receive capacity, not evidence that512bytes were actually written; the intended helper would submit exactly2 payload bytes.

This makes the invariant precise: **after any ambiguous header or payload result, submit no further OUT transfer, including read headers, restore headers, mode/reset headers, retries, fallback-bank requests or another tool's vendor command.** A later12-byte header could otherwise be consumed as pending memory data. Canceling a host operation, releasing/closing its FD or deleting the stage is not evidence that the device's pending receive state has been reset. Parent coordination must carry the unresolved phase forward and exclude competing clients.

There are different failure cases. If the fixed header is refused/ambiguous and no payload is sent, the device may be waiting for data; the helper must not guess. If its only data attempt is the bounded payload`0100` or`0000`, the intended unresolved RAM effect is the one lane word/low byte, and it may remain installed even though the receipt cannot confirm it. Readback/receipt/close failure after exact payload also leaves an unresolved runtime intervention. The wider armed receive descriptor is why confinement relies on exact buffer lengths **and no later OUT**, not on a claim that firmware enforces a2-byte receive ceiling in every failure case.

Nothing in this qualified normal packet selects executable memory, persistent media, boot configuration, networking configuration or a storage command. No direct code/boot/network corruption mechanism is identified for that fixed source path. It is technically different from bricking firmware. However, the altered SRAM word affects a protective comparison; electrical harmlessness, physical lane meaning and consequences of a prolonged skip are not proven. Driver/DMA faults, a wrong live image, other clients and violating the pending-OUT invariant are outside the normal-packet confinement argument.

Accordingly, lack of guaranteed immediate restore is a real runtime-state limitation, **not by itself a universal ban on implementing a finite volatile diagnostic**. A future fixed helper can fail closed, preserve an unresolved ownership/phase receipt and stop all vendor activity. Its verified ordinary restore is reversible on the admitted normal path; it is not an unconditional rollback guarantee. Unknown-phase re-entry remains unqualified and cannot be improvised after failure. Any later recovery must be independently source/transport-qualified for that exact state. MODE2 is not a mask restore, and startup source zeroing is not proof that a particular reset crosses both required initialization domains. No recovery operation is authorized or implemented by this review.

## Model coverage versus future implementation requirements

The transfer model validates the basic phases and refuses a second header after ambiguity. Its fresh/post contexts enforce the full windows and source invariants. Fault RT1/4097/0 can still admit a separate restore, so the intervention does not become permanently owned merely because a fault appeared. Both source schedule arithmetic and32second pre-write reserve are correct **conditional on** the2second bulk timeouts being honored. Release/close and root45s/host75s bounds are not hard native termination proofs.

The model is intentionally smaller than a helper. It has no transport adapter, immutable native receipt loader, one-write process budget, ownership-ledger consumption, explicit restore-confirmation state, serializer bound or real clock/exception/cleanup implementation. In particular, the generic `Transfer` allows another header after a completed write and accepts caller-chosen readback expectations; `confirm_set` still requires post-context word1, but does not itself bind the generic readback's expected value. Independent tests exercise these gaps. They are not contradictions of the design's no-helper status, but copying this model verbatim into production would not enforce the promised contract. A future implementation must fix/bind these states and independently test them.

## Sufficient next qualification, in order

1. Implement and independently review a **read-only** fixed five-region fingerprint reader using the qualified current native claim/FD/descriptors and pending-pair rules. Collect only those324bytes, compare every fixed hash/literal, stop on mismatch/short/ambiguity, retain original refusal outcomes and prove bounded receipts. No write follows automatically from its success.
2. Produce a separate execute-disabled fixed2B volatile-write implementation and complete host adversarial tests. Its allowlist must admit only the exact16-read/one-write schedule; prove exact payload buffer length, pre-header versus pending-partner deadline behavior, first-error retention, Unknown hard stop across wrappers/tools, resource cleanup, exclusive bounded receipts, original/installed full-window ledger and separate single-consumption restore/readback. This is ordinary safety engineering, not a demand for absolute all-writer proof.
3. Before a centrally coordinated diagnostic, qualify current source/context/table originals and the known-writer stability hypothesis, limit the exposure and determine the accepted residual runtime ownership risk. Preserve all failed admissions. Treat a same-value stock publication as unobservable with this evidence. Compare epoch/current references/tuple premises and earlier-lane termination rather than taking a clean panel or absent570tuple as causal proof.

These steps can support review of a narrowly bounded runtime experiment. They do not qualify a permanent fix, optical safety, guaranteed all-failure restoration or any currently absent helper. Current next acquisition is step1 only.
