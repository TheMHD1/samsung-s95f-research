# Prospective wake-window capture design

The smallest recorder change is option A, a **wrapper-only read-only cached Live+saver checkpoint with no pre-native key** and unchanged FN native DLL/parser/key worker. It is the preferred experiment only after its initial condition is independently available. That initial condition is **not necessarily constructible using the currently qualified Apps recovery path**. For the presently recovered Apps starting state, option B is more directly constructible, but requires a separately qualified native/parser derivative. Neither option should be presented as ready for execution from this design.

FW is the motivating negative control. Its prior EB baseline was clean40/logger0, but after separate Live preparation, staging and another ordinary wake the pre-arm R was already faulted. FU correctly refused before logger arm or Left. The native sampled-state gap was 288733304 us, not a tight wake-onset bracket. The experiment identifies no causal preparation step, transition destination or physical condition. Extending recording after Left cannot recover that missed pre-arm interval.

## A: arm before the first prospective Live wake

Retain FN's exact native, fixed read/write packets, one owned FD, binding, clean pre-arm checks, logger0→1→0 semantics, first full clean scan1/C8=3 baseline, one native Left down/up, pending-pair obligations, clock/error rules, 56 rows, 58-byte layout, 25 ms period, 2-second nominal window and restoration budget. Do not send Up, VOLUP, Left or navigation through the wrapper. Replace the entire post-staging wake workflow, rather than just modifying its accepted predicate, with one bounded read-only cached snapshot workflow.

The new workflow runs after staging/self-test/census, immediately before the native command. It requires the canonical unique Live tab, CSFS present and saver windows present, complete boot/uptime framing, source-pinned getters and fresh identity. It may take a finite at-most-ten-query settle under a 10-second cooperative budget; no key or automatic fallback is allowed. Every query and response is retained. A final accepted query must precede native root submission by at most 1 second in the host monotonic clock, as in FN. No claim is made about root latency or continuous saver state during native startup. A missing saver, changed tab, malformed response, failed clock or stale submission stops without native invocation.

The native then reads the baseline ring and fresh R/M/C, arms only after its existing Clean40/logger0/context guards, verifies armed R, and completes its first clean cycle before starting its sole Left worker. The intervention is explicitly **intended saver wake from cached Live**, not a proved Live→ForYou exposure. Successful key children, a key overlap witness, later cached UI and any physical report are separate facts. The wrapper reports zero UI operations; the native reports its own one planned Left workflow. No FU UpUp preparation certificate or source-to-destination header claim applies to A.

A does not capture any fault that develops before arm/first clean cycle. In that case it must preserve an initial-clean or first-cycle refusal, no guessed re-arm, no automatic reset and no retry. Retain FN's initial C8=3/scan1 guards; a clean-looking saver display does not qualify these inputs. Finite later C8=0..4/scan0..1 and Quiescent recording remain unchanged. Unknown phase never permits another header, including restoration; accepted headers still receive their matching IN/payload even on clock error. A has the same stock event ring and logger side effects as FN and no threshold or executable write.

### Constructing the premise is a separate problem

There is no qualified ScreenSaver launcher/service command in this scoped source set, and none is proposed. EL is positive history for cached Apps+saver and a saver-intended Left observation, not proof that a saver can safely be started on Live or that a fault latch clears in saver. FW positively records cached Live+saver, but not a contemporaneous clean R under that saver. The firmware latch can remain set after a display/context change; waiting alone cannot be assumed to restore admission.

A bounded passive preparation may wait for a naturally appearing saver on an already normally selected Live tab, for example a separate 120-second idle interval with at most five cached-only checks, stopping at the first required snapshot. This is a proposed finite waiting contract, not a known saver timeout or a way to set it. The wait occurs before USB claim and outside the native 40-second budget. Failed appearance or faulted pre-arm is an unavailable premise, not permission to wake/retry/relax the native guard. Entering Live from Apps may itself cross the fault trigger before this point; this is the important setup gap.

A distinct future prerequisite could initialize under **cached Live+saver** without waking or navigating: require a fresh, accepted, stable EB fault40/logger0 and exact known terminal/cleanup provenance; require cached Live+saver/CSFS before and after; retain all current public reject gates, stock pins, fixed operation41/mode2, unchanged deadline helper and one-attempt/no-retry semantics. After the one stock invocation, require positive old-instance disconnect/new-instance enumeration with descriptors/same boot, unbound/no holders and fresh EB. Only actual fresh Clean40/logger0 plus FN's full M/C first-baseline requirements can admit A. If still faulted, saver disappears, C8/scan does not qualify, or any identity/phase/transport condition is ambiguous, stop. Do not send an automatic wake to manufacture success.

This is a separate **Live+saver stock MODE2 reinitialization experiment**, not the existing Apps recovery quick path with a guard silently removed. Source explains the distinction: BV's README traces Apps to the owner-observed populated-Apps recovery sequence; EI's recover-eb-fault.py performs navigation and `apps_gate` above the fixed helper. DH's fixed helper contract is operation41/mode2 with a 12-second native deadline and stock file checks, without an Apps/current-tab condition. The lower helper source does not establish equal effectiveness or safety across UI contexts. Historical MODE2 GPIO/reset/USB/I2C replay proves a stock lifecycle, not all-RAM clearing, instruction reload, cold boot or optical recovery. The Apps policy's historical role is not an intrinsic tab check in the fixed ioctl ABI; the candidate Live premise still requires its own source/review/runtime disposition. No such derivative is implemented here.

## B: extend the Apps→Live observation cap, retaining the no-saver setup

Use the known Apps preparation and ordinary wrapper wake, then the same native first-full-clean baseline and one Left. This directly starts from the currently qualified recovery baseline. A fixed cap81 derivative is sufficient to remove cap56 as the reason for stopping before the nominal 2-second deadline: 80 nominal 25-ms inter-cycle waits alone reach 2 seconds. It does not guarantee 81 full cycles, equal cadence or a full accepted 2-second run. USB/clock/key scheduling consumes additional time; a deadline can be reached in the middle of the eight-read set. Existing `TimeLimitedPartial` remains refused with truthful retained data and guarded restoration. Do not silently promote a partial run or assert that missing an event in a shorter interval excludes the mechanism.

Necessary source changes are explicit: Protocol.Config.MaxCycles, ScoreCheckpoints capacity, Engine's fixed maximum, capture-cycle and row limits in the native parser, all counter bound checks, wrapper manifest/dry descriptions, lower decoder or admission limits wherever the frozen native family is consumed, generated fixtures, unique family schemas and assembly/checkpoint/payload pins. NativeBinding, OnsetKey, fixed specs, ReadPair/WriteLogger, logger guard profile, cleanup admission, key overlap qualifier and action bytes should remain exact. Receipt capacity and output framing stay fixed; no extra UI action or background task is added.

For81 complete cycles with two logger payloads, the existing accounting becomes headers660, inputs658, writes2 and bulks1320. FN's parser currently bounds numeric counters at1000; changing only the row cap would therefore reject an otherwise valid long run. Set precise derivative counter maxima from the new fixed accounting, not an unbounded general integer policy. The maximal observed row payload grows from3248 raw/4332 base64 bytes to4698 raw/6264 base64 bytes, a1932-byte serialized row-string increase. FN's constructive stress receipt is18273bytes; adding only that row string yields20205, below24576, but this is a planning estimate, **not a proof of the new worst receipt**. Counter widths, retained-cycle numbers, maximum errors/clocks and success/refusal branch metadata must be included in a new constructive serializer test and envelope/cmd-cap tests. Ring sidecars remain outside the native receipt.

The 40-second cooperative budget, 26-second restoration reserve, 7-second key join reserve, 2-second per-bulk timeout, root45/host75 seconds, command8192, root response65536, structured receipt24576 and two15372-byte rings remain unchanged. Each optional pair still needs a fresh admission check; larger nominal transfer totals are not a license to start an unbounded cycle while armed. A full81-cycle success has660 pairs,1320 transfers; the possible worst per-pair runtime is limited by admission, not by claiming all could finish inside40seconds. All accepted-header completion and known/unknown-phase rules remain unchanged.

## Recommendation and qualification work

Do not run A merely because a saver window is visible after a Live fault: its clean pre-arm premise may be unavailable. If a fresh clean Live+saver premise becomes available without a new uncontrolled wake, A is the smallest useful next capture because its native/packets are unchanged and it moves the intended wake inside the armed observation. Otherwise B is the practical direct successor from recovered Apps; it improves temporal coverage but tests the Apps→Live path rather than the missed Live wake. A future Live+saver MODE2 prerequisite is a separately reviewed route to make A constructible; it is not assumed to work.

A's focused tests must prove no wrapper key bytes/request, exact inherited native/parser payloads, missing/duplicate/wrong cached tabs, saver absent, CSFS absent, malformed/oversize snapshots, bounded query exhaustion, stale/future clocks, known pre-arm fault with zero key/arm, first-cycle non3/no Left with guarded logger cleanup, and exact original refusal preservation/no follow-up requests. A clean baseline and completed key after arm plus optional-R overlap are necessary for a wake-exposure qualifier; post UI is independent and has no proof of intermediate continuity.

B's tests must cover exact81 cap allocation, counter maxima and representative short/clock boundary accounting, all58-byte row/source bindings, constructive maximum receipt and root envelopes, old FN refusal of81/counter overflow, and unchanged key/pending-pair/binding functions. Test TimeLimit and TimeLimitedPartial separately, and keep the latter refused even when logger restoration succeeds. Neither option establishes physical light, actual executing PC/FP, causality, whole-image identity, or a root-cause fix. No code/helper implementation, TV, root transport, network, deployment or frozen-source change was performed for this design.
