## Deeper logging work kept separate

FD resolved the kernel vlog provider and a distinct internal TCON log reader.
Its one fresh-open4096-byte request/allocation is source-bounded; smaller
reads violate the handler's count contract. The handler omits its header and
ignores copy-to-user residuals, so complete-log acquisition remains unproved.
FF traced a SoC boot-control handoff of the TCON-info DMA base. Neither audit
has established the receiving producer or a bridge to external RTP. No new
debugfs reader was deployed by this trial.

FG is a host-only combined stock-event/checkpoint recorder prototype under
development. It is not frozen, reviewed or deployed. Its intended single
owner would sample the stock event ring while recording state and restore
the logging gate, with bounded optional reads and reserved cleanup budget.
It can add an event argument, not live comparison FP, selected-A or caller
LR. Exact DS hook sites remain a separate source-directed task requiring
owned execution storage, safe install/removal and instruction visibility.
