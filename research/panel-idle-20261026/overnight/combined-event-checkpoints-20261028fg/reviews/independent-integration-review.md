There are at most twelve control pairs: baseline ring; prearm R/M/C; arm; armed R; prerestore M/C/R; restore; restored R; post ring. Prearm requires the logger halfword exactly0 and clean known RT. The final prerestore R is nearest the write. Exactly1 allows the fixed clear; exactly0 is a distinct observed already-zero/no-write outcome. Upper-bit drift, unknown RT/profile, wrong root/AA/AC/geometry, or invalid scan refuses the write. There is no blind clear, automatic retry, reset, fallback, persistent operation or executable patch. The M/C/R sampling and write are sequential; an intervening stock writer is not excluded, and a readback is not a guarantee against later rearming.

A full accepted read header always gets its one matching IN, including after a clock exception. A full logger header similarly gets its one matching 2-byte OUT payload. Short/exceptional transfers leave Unknown and prohibit new headers. Clock errors with completed transport still stop later headers. Release and close are reconciled separately.
