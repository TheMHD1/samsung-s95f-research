## Corrections to preserve during handoff

The root-source report's first draft treated target boot applicability too
generally. The exact S95F already launched a replacement Gaming Hub managed
assembly at UID 5001 with no effective capabilities, denied its child dotnet
execution, and returned zero bytes in the tested loopback SDB exchange. The
original assembly was restored. The fork uses managed staging and a socket
request, so child-execution denial alone does not refute it. Start with those
existing results rather than assuming either success or impossibility. See
the independent audit's source excerpts and target-artifact links.

Related S95F capture/decryption results remain attributed to their author.
The identical fork documents are one lineage. The target firmware's section
names, salts, plaintext CRCs, installed-image correspondence and signatures
are not supplied by matching interval lengths or by an EU manifest.

The current recovery remains the Apps-selected single stock controller restart.
Do not change that working baseline merely to test an unrelated launcher,
root exploit, capture application or boot module. Any future prevention claim
must survive the original For You/Live and independent video cases; a clean
Apps-only control is insufficient.
